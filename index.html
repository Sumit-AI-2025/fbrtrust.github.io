Varanasi, India

Turning displaced bulls into a self-sustaining rural economy

FBR Trust rehabilitates abandoned bulls and cows through the Nandi Ghar model — combining animal care with biogas, solar power, organic farming and dairy logistics to create clean energy and dignified livelihoods for rural India.

The Nandi Ghar model

Six pillars, one integrated system

Every animal we shelter supports a cycle of energy, soil health and local employment — so care for the animals pays for itself over time.

🐂
Nandi Ghar
Scientific gaushala with shelter, fodder and full veterinary care
⚡
Bio-CNG & Biogas
Cattle dung converted into clean fuel and organic manure
🔆
Bull Power (BPGS)
Bull-powered generation units producing clean energy at zero fuel cost
☀️
Solar Power
Solar generation aligned with PM-KUSUM for on-site use and grid supply
🌾
Organic Farming
Vermicompost and natural farming to restore soil and grow clean produce
🏍️
Milk Collection
Doorstep dairy logistics and micro-franchise jobs for rural youth
