About & Mission

Every bull deserves a place and a purpose

The Foundation for Bull Rehabilitation & Charitable Trust (FBR Trust) is a public charitable trust established in 2023, registered in Gujarat and working on the ground in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. We exist because thousands of bulls and unproductive cattle are abandoned every year, left to wander roads and fields without food, shelter or care.

🎯

Our Mission

To rescue, shelter and care for abandoned bulls and cattle, and to make that care financially self-sustaining by turning the resources they produce into clean energy, organic agriculture and rural employment.

🌅

Our Vision

A rural India where no animal is abandoned, where every gaushala is a productive community asset, and where young people find dignified work close to home.

How we work

The Nandi Ghar approach

Most shelters depend entirely on donations and struggle to survive. Nandi Ghar is designed differently — each part of the system supports the others.

  1. Rescue & careAbandoned bulls and cattle receive shelter, fodder and regular veterinary attention.
  2. Energy from resourcesDung feeds biogas and Bio-CNG units; bull-powered generators and solar panels add clean electricity.
  3. Healthy soilSlurry and vermicompost enrich organic farms, reducing chemical use and producing clean food.
  4. Local livelihoodsOperations, farming and dairy collection create jobs for rural youth and women.
  5. Self-sustaining careIncome from energy, compost and produce flows back to fund the animals' care.
