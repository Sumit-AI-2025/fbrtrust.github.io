Flagship · Proposal submitted

Nandi Ghar at Kanha Upvan Nandishala

Kanha Upvan Nandishala at Bhitkuri, Varanasi is an existing gaushala owned by Nagar Nigam Varanasi. FBR Trust and partner Shri Surbhi Sankool have proposed to jointly adopt, rehabilitate and operate it as the first Nandi Ghar site.

The site was inspected in September 2026, and a summary proposal has been submitted to the Government of Uttar Pradesh through the UP Gau Seva Aayog and the District Magistrate, Varanasi. A detailed project report is being prepared for trustees, donors and CSR partners.

Animal care

Upgraded sheds, clean water, fodder management and a regular veterinary schedule.

Resource use

Biogas from dung, vermicompost units and on-site organic cultivation.

Community

Local employment and training for operations, farming and dairy work.

Upcoming · Pilot project

NEAA — An FBR Trust Project

Northeast Remote Accounting Academy is a capacity-building pilot of FBR Trust for final-year B.Com and M.Com students from the eight Northeastern states. It aims to close the gap between classroom commerce and the practical accounting skills that offices and clients actually use, and to help students prepare for remote accounting work.

This is a first, experimental batch. We will learn from it, share honest results, and improve the programme before expanding.

Please read before you apply

  • No job guarantee. Completing the programme does not guarantee employment or income. Any remote work opportunity depends on a student's assessment results, eligibility and verification, and on the actual availability of client work and vacancies at that time.
  • Pilot programme. Course content, schedule and dates may change as we learn from the first batch.
  • Fees are separate from donations. Pay the programme fee only as stated in your official admission details. Never pay programme fees into FBR Trust's donation account or UPI. Programme fees are not donations and no 80G receipt applies.
  • Your data. Student details are used only to run the programme. They will be shared with NSPL for work consideration only with the student's written consent.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a laptop?
Yes. A laptop or desktop with a stable internet connection is needed for practical software work. Classes can be watched on a phone.

Will I get a certificate?
Students who complete the programme and pass the assessment receive a certificate of completion from FBR Trust. It is not a government or university qualification.

Is the programme accredited?
No. It is a practical skill programme run as a pilot by FBR Trust.

Can my college partner with you?
Yes. Colleges can host a free orientation session for their students. Use the button below.

Programme areas

What the model includes

🐂 Core

Bull & cattle rehabilitation

Rescue of abandoned bulls and unproductive cattle, with shelter, fodder, treatment and lifelong care.

⚡ Planned

Biogas & Bio-CNG

Converting cattle dung into cooking gas and compressed bio-gas, with slurry returned to farms as manure. Aligned with GOBARDHAN and SATAT.

🔆 Planned

Bull Power Generation (BPGS)

Bull-driven generator units that give working bulls a purpose while producing electricity without fuel.

☀️ Planned

Solar energy

Solar installations for on-site power and grid supply, structured under PM-KUSUM and MNRE programmes.

🌾 Planned

Organic farming

Vermicompost production and natural farming that improves soil health and supplies fodder and clean produce. Aligned with PKVY.

🏍️ Planned

Rural livelihoods & dairy

Doorstep milk collection and skill training that create work for local youth, aligned with PMKVY and Rashtriya Gokul Mission.
