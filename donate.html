For companies

Partner with us through CSR

Our work qualifies under Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013 — including animal welfare, environmental sustainability, rural development and livelihood enhancement. We offer structured, well-documented partnerships with clear reporting.

Project sponsorship

Fund a defined part of a Nandi Ghar site — sheds, a biogas unit, solar panels or a veterinary unit.

Animal adoption programme

Sponsor the care of a set number of animals for a year, with periodic updates and photos.

Livelihood & skills

Support training and employment for rural youth in farming, energy and dairy operations.

What CSR partners receive

  • Utilisation certificate and audited accounts for the funded project
  • Quarterly progress reports with photographs and impact data
  • 80G receipt where applicable, and site visits on request
